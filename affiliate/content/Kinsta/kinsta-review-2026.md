## H2: Honest Cons

- **Premium pricing**: this is meaningfully more expensive than budget/shared WordPress hosting, and the gap is a real consideration if your site doesn't generate revenue or isn't business-critical
- **Visit-based pricing model**: pricing scales with traffic, and sites that unexpectedly spike in visits can trigger overage charges — model your realistic traffic (including any hoped-for growth or viral spikes) against the plan limits before committing
- **No cPanel**: if you or your team specifically rely on cPanel workflows/plugins, the switch to MyKinsta requires adjustment
- **WordPress-only focus**: if you need to host non-WordPress sites too, you'll be managing a separate hosting relationship or using Kinsta's separate application-hosting product
- **No email hosting**: like most managed WordPress hosts, Kinsta doesn't include email hosting — budget for a separate email provider (e.g., Google Workspace) for @yourdomain email
- **Overkill for low-traffic/hobby sites**: a small personal blog with minimal traffic doesn't need premium container infrastructure — cheaper hosting will do the job fine
- **No hosting "guarantees" a fast, ranking site**: Kinsta removes hosting as a bottleneck; it doesn't fix a bloated theme, unoptimized images, or poor content strategy
