## Short answer

The cost of a Webflow website depends on the site you want to publish, how you manage it, and any outside services you choose. Start with the appropriate site subscription, then account for your domain, optional tools and any paid design or development work.

Use this as a budgeting framework, not a price quote. For current prices and plan limits, **verify on webflow.com**: [official pricing](https://webflow.com/pricing). Compare the amount payable today with the renewal cost — not just the advertised monthly equivalent.

## The 3 costs that make up "a Webflow website"

### 1. The Site plan — and whether you also need a paid Workspace

A Site plan covers an individual hosted website. A Workspace concerns how you build and manage sites. They are different purchases: a paid Workspace does not replace the Site plan needed to host a custom-domain site with Webflow. Do not assume you need a paid Workspace merely because you are publishing one website. [Plan overview](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961379128723-Webflow-Workspace-and-Site-plans-overview)

For content managed through Webflow Collections, choose a plan with the CMS capabilities you need. Basic is positioned for sites without a CMS. Check the current names and allowances rather than relying on an older CMS/Business pricing table: **verify on webflow.com**. [Pricing](https://webflow.com/pricing)

Before choosing, list what the site actually needs: static pages, structured articles, forms, collaboration and expected usage. A blog is one possible content strategy; it is not automatically necessary for every website.

### 2. Ecommerce — an alternative Site plan for native checkout

If you need Webflow's native Ecommerce checkout, evaluate its Ecommerce plans. Do not automatically add both a general Site subscription and a second Ecommerce subscription for the same site: Ecommerce is a Site-plan option. [Choose a Site plan](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961232582419-Choose-a-Site-plan)

Before budgeting, check applicable platform transaction fees, payment-provider fees, catalog requirements and any outside services. Exact charges and current limits: **verify on webflow.com**, then check the chosen payment provider's official terms.

For a store, compare the workflows you actually need — products, payments and order handling — rather than assuming that either Webflow or a competing platform is always the better choice.

### 3. Your domain — and optional work or services

Treat domain registration as a separate budget item. Webflow supports connecting an existing domain and offers a purchase route through IONOS. [Domain preparation](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961388027539-Prepare-to-connect-your-domain-to-Webflow)

Check the actual registrar quote, renewal price and any premium-domain charge. There is no reliable universal annual price to insert here.

Also list optional costs only if you need them: a template, professional design, content production, business email or third-party tools. Separate one-time work from recurring subscriptions so your first-year estimate does not become your assumed renewal budget.

## What you DON'T pay extra for (Webflow's advantage)

For a site hosted with Webflow, hosting is part of the Site-plan purchase. Webflow also documents its SSL hosting and certificate renewal process. [Site plans](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961232582419-Choose-a-Site-plan), [SSL hosting](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961362849811-SSL-hosting)

That can simplify the list of services you manage, but it does not mean every website cost or maintenance task disappears. Budget separately for the work and integrations your project requires.

A fair WordPress comparison needs an actual hosting package and feature list. Do not assume that every WordPress site requires paid plugins, or that Webflow will always be cheaper or more predictable.

## Is there a free way to start?

Webflow currently lists a free Starter option with a webflow.io domain and limited features. It is useful for evaluating the builder, but should not be described as an unrestricted way to build any complete site before paying. Custom-domain publishing requires an appropriate paid Site plan. **Verify on webflow.com** for current limits. [Pricing](https://webflow.com/pricing)

A practical test is to build a small representative page or content sample first. Decide what you need to validate before investing time in the whole site.

## Honest downsides on cost

- **Your content model affects your options.** Check CMS requirements before choosing a plan solely on its headline price.
- **The base subscription may not be the whole budget.** Review the allowances, collaboration needs and optional services relevant to your project.
- **Leaving can require rebuilding functionality.** Webflow's code-export documentation says CMS and Ecommerce databases/functionality are not included in exported code. Plan migration around the features you use, not an assumption of a complete one-click transfer. [Code export](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961386739347-How-do-I-export-my-Webflow-site-code)

## FAQ

**Is Webflow free?**
A free Starter option is listed, with limits. Use it to assess your needs; **verify on webflow.com** before planning around a particular feature or allowance. [Pricing](https://webflow.com/pricing)

**Why not compare it directly with cheap WordPress hosting?**
A hosting headline alone is not a like-for-like website budget. Compare the actual services, paid tools and work required for the same project. Neither option is automatically cheaper.

**Do I need a CMS-capable plan?**
If your design relies on Webflow Collections, check the appropriate CMS-capable option. Publishing useful content does not by itself mean every page must use Collections. [Plan overview](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961379128723-Webflow-Workspace-and-Site-plans-overview)

**Can I use my own domain?**
Yes, with an appropriate paid Site plan. Budget for the domain separately and follow the connection instructions. [Domain preparation](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961388027539-Prepare-to-connect-your-domain-to-Webflow)

**Do I need both a paid Site plan and a paid Workspace?**
Not automatically. Determine whether your building and collaboration needs require a paid Workspace separately from the site's hosting needs. [Plan overview](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961379128723-Webflow-Workspace-and-Site-plans-overview)

**What should I check before choosing annual billing?**
Compare the full upfront payment, monthly alternative, renewal terms and cancellation/refund policy. Confirm current terms on the official site before committing; this guide does not promise a refund or a particular discount.
