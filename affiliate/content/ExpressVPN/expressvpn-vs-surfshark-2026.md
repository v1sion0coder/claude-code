## What neither VPN can do (read this before you buy either one)

Marketing in the VPN space often overpromises. To be direct about the limits of this entire category of product, regardless of provider:

- **Neither service makes you "anonymous" online.** They hide your IP address from the sites you visit and encrypt your traffic to their server, but the provider can technically see your connection metadata, and any account you log into (email, social media, banking) still identifies you regardless of VPN use.
- **Neither guarantees bypassing every block or restriction.** Streaming platforms and some networks actively detect and block known VPN IP ranges; what works today may stop working after a platform update.
- **A VPN doesn't protect you from phishing, malware, weak passwords, or a compromised account.** It's one layer of network-level protection, not a complete security solution.
- **No-logs claims depend on trusting the provider and the scope of their audits** — they meaningfully reduce risk versus not using a VPN at all, but they aren't an absolute guarantee, for either provider.
